About This Game

Onet Master is a timed tile-connection puzzle developed by Potato Jam and published on Poki. Select two identical pictures that can be joined by an open route, then continue until the board is empty. Gameul provides instructions and links to the official Poki game page.

How to Play

  1. Open Onet Master on the official Poki website.
  2. Select one picture tile, then choose an identical tile with an unobstructed connection path.
  3. Keep removing pairs and clear the full board before the timer reaches zero.

Controls

  • Click or tap the first tile
  • Choose an identical tile that can be connected by a clear path
  • Remove all matching pairs before time runs out

Tips and Strategies

  • Start with obvious pairs around the outside edge where routes are easier to see.
  • Remove blocking tiles to open new paths through the middle.
  • Scan one picture type at a time instead of searching the whole board randomly.
