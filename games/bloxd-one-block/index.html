About This Game

Bloxd One Block is a sky-island building challenge from Bloxd.io. Your world begins with very little space, so every block and item matters as you gather materials and turn a small platform into a larger base.

How to Play

  1. Enter the One Block mode on the official Bloxd.io screen.
  2. Break the central renewing block to collect new materials.
  3. Place blocks carefully to enlarge the platform, then build storage and useful structures.

Controls

  • Use WASD or the on-screen controls to move
  • Use the mouse or touch controls to break and place blocks
  • Press Space to jump and use the inventory to manage resources

Tips and Strategies

  • Add a safety border before carrying rare materials near the edge.
  • Keep spare blocks in storage so one fall does not stop your progress.
  • Expand one stable level before building upward.
