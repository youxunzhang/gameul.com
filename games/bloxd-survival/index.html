About This Game

Bloxd Survival is a multiplayer SMP mode provided by Bloxd.io. Explore a persistent block world, gather ores, craft equipment, build a base and protect your land while sharing the server with other players.

How to Play

  1. Choose a lobby or create a private world on the official Bloxd.io screen.
  2. Collect basic blocks and resources before crafting tools and armour.
  3. Build away from busy spawn areas, protect your land and return regularly to maintain your base.

Controls

  • Use WASD or the on-screen controls to move
  • Move the mouse to look around and use the mouse buttons to interact
  • Press Space to jump and open the in-game inventory to craft

Tips and Strategies

  • Create simple tools first, then search for stronger ores deeper underground.
  • Store valuable items safely before exploring dangerous areas.
  • Only give trusted players access to a protected base.

Frequently Asked Questions
