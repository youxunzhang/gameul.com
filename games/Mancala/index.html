🎯 Objective

The goal of Mancala is to collect more stones in your store (the large pit on your right) than your opponent by the end of the game.

🧩 Game Setup

  • The board has two rows of six pits each.
  • Each small pit starts with four stones.
  • The large pits (stores) at each end belong to the players.
  • You control the six pits on your side and the store on your right.

🔄 Gameplay

  • On your turn, pick up all stones from one of your pits.
  • Move counterclockwise, dropping one stone into each pit as you go.
  • Skip your opponent’s store, but place stones in your own store when passing it.
  • If your last stone lands in your own store, you get another turn.
  • If your last stone lands in an empty pit on your side, you capture that stone and any stones in the opposite pit, placing them in your store.

🛑 Game End

The game ends when one side’s six pits are empty. The player with stones remaining on their side collects them into their store. The winner is the one with the most stones in their store.

💡 Tips for Winning

Try to end your turn in your store to earn an extra move.
Watch your opponent’s moves to avoid letting them capture.
Plan ahead — sometimes skipping a big capture now leads to a better one later.
