About This Game

Ellie and Friends Pre Fall Outfit is a casual fashion game from Cutedressup. Build seasonal looks for Ellie and her friends using layered clothing, accessories and warm pre-fall colors.

How to Play

  1. Choose a character to style.
  2. Select clothing, hairstyles and accessories using the on-screen controls.
  3. Mix different pieces until you complete a pre-fall look you like.

Controls

  • Use the left mouse button on desktop
  • Tap clothing and accessories on mobile
  • Follow the in-game prompts to complete each look

Tips and Strategies

  • Start with one color palette before adding accessories.
  • Combine lighter layers with one bold seasonal item.
  • Try each category to discover all available outfit pieces.
