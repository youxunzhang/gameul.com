About This Game

Talking Tom Gold Run is an endless runner by Outfit7. Tom chases Roy through streets full of vehicles and barriers, collecting gold to improve homes and unlock more adventures. Gameul offers a guide and a link to the Poki game page.

How to Play

  1. Open Talking Tom Gold Run on Poki.
  2. Switch between the three lanes, jump over barriers and slide under low obstacles.
  3. Collect gold while avoiding traffic and try to extend each run.

Controls

  • Use left and right arrow keys to change lanes
  • Press the up arrow to jump and the down arrow to slide
  • On mobile, swipe left, right, up or down

Tips and Strategies

  • Look at the next obstacle before chasing a line of gold.
  • Choose an open lane early when several barriers appear together.
  • Get comfortable with jumping and sliding before making rapid lane changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who developed Talking Tom Gold Run?

Talking Tom Gold Run is developed by Outfit7.

Can I use touch controls?

Yes. Swipe left or right to switch lanes, up to jump and down to slide on mobile.
