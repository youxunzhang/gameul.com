About This Game

Tic Tac Toe is a compact strategy game for two players. Take turns placing X and O, then try to complete a row before your opponent can block it.

How to Play

  1. Choose whether to play the computer or another person.
  2. Place your mark in any empty square.
  3. Make three in a row horizontally, vertically or diagonally to win.

Controls

  • Click or tap an empty square
  • Choose Computer or Two Players
  • Select New Round to clear the board

Tips and Strategies

  • The centre creates the most possible winning lines.
  • Always block an opponent who has two marks in a row.
  • Create two possible winning lines at once to force a victory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can two people play on one device?

Yes. Choose Two Players and take turns using the same board.
