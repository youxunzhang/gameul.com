About This Game

Panda: Bubble Shooter is an arcade puzzle game developed by LAK Games and published on Poki. Plan the angle of each shot, connect bubbles of the same color and remove clusters to free the trapped little pandas. Gameul provides this guide and links to the official Poki game page.

How to Play

  1. Open the game on the official Poki website.
  2. Hold the mouse button or your finger and adjust the aiming line.
  3. Release to fire, match three or more bubbles of one color and clear each level.

Controls

  • Hold the mouse button or touch the screen to aim
  • Release to launch the bubble
  • Match at least three bubbles of the same color to clear them

Tips and Strategies

  • Use wall rebounds when a direct shot is blocked.
  • Target bubbles that support a larger hanging group.
  • Check the next bubble color before choosing a difficult angle.
