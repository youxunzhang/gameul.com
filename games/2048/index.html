About This Game

2048 is a calm but challenging number-merging puzzle. Every move shifts the entire board, so successful games depend on planning ahead and protecting open space.

How to Play

  1. Move all tiles in one direction.
  2. Tiles with the same value merge when they meet.
  3. Create the 2048 tile before the board fills up.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or WASD on desktop
  • Swipe on touch screens
  • Use New Game to restart

Tips and Strategies

  • Keep your largest tile in one corner.
  • Avoid moving in all four directions without a plan.
  • Preserve at least one open row or column for recovery.
