About This Game

Temple Run 2 is an endless running game by Imangi Studios. Follow twisting paths through ancient ruins, react to traps and collect coins as the pace increases. Gameul provides a guide before you continue to the game on Poki.

How to Play

  1. Open Temple Run 2 on Poki.
  2. Jump over gaps, slide under low obstacles and turn when the path changes direction.
  3. Collect coins when the route is clear and survive as long as you can.

Controls

  • Press the up arrow to jump and the down arrow to slide
  • Use left and right arrows to turn or change lanes
  • On mobile, swipe in the direction of the action

Tips and Strategies

  • Watch the path ahead so sharp turns do not catch you by surprise.
  • Prioritize a safe route over a risky coin pickup.
  • Time each action for the obstacle in front of you instead of pressing repeatedly.
