About This Game

Obby Parkour Race: Multiplayer is a DAPALAB game available on CrazyGames. Players compete on obstacle courses with narrow platforms and tricky routes, while collectible pets add another reason to explore. Gameul provides a guide and a link to play on CrazyGames.

How to Play

  1. Open the CrazyGames page with the play button.
  2. Follow the course, cross obstacles and stay on the platforms.
  3. Keep a steady pace and reach the finish ahead of other players.

Controls

  • Use WASD to move
  • Hold Shift to run and use the left mouse button to attack
  • On mobile, use the left joystick and on-screen action buttons

Tips and Strategies

  • Line up with the center of a narrow platform before moving forward.
  • Learn a safe route before trying to run it faster.
  • Watch the next obstacle rather than looking only at your character.
