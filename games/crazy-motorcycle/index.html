About This Game

Crazy Motorcycle is an arcade driving game developed by AM and published on CrazyGames. Steer a block-style motorcycle rider along narrow floating tracks, avoid moving hazards, collect rewards and use checkpoints while working through multiple worlds. Gameul provides this guide and links to the official CrazyGames page.

How to Play

  1. Open Crazy Motorcycle on the official CrazyGames website.
  2. Steer carefully along the track and line up the motorcycle before each jump.
  3. Cross gaps, avoid obstacles, collect rewards and reach the finish line to complete the stage.

Controls

  • Use WASD or the arrow keys to steer and control the motorcycle
  • Press Space to jump across gaps
  • Use the left mouse button to interact with menus

Tips and Strategies

  • Use small steering movements near narrow edges instead of turning sharply.
  • Build enough speed before a wide gap, then jump from the center of the ramp.
  • Slow down around moving obstacles and use checkpoints to protect your progress.
