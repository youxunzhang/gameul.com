About This Game

Bloxd BedWars is a competitive team mode from Bloxd.io. Teams protect their own bed while collecting supplies, improving equipment and building routes toward rival islands. Lose the bed and future defeats become final.

How to Play

  1. Join a BedWars match and identify your team's bed and resource area.
  2. Collect resources, obtain useful gear and build defenses around the bed.
  3. Cross to rival islands, destroy enemy beds and eliminate the remaining players.

Controls

  • Use WASD or the on-screen controls to move
  • Use the mouse buttons to attack, mine and place blocks
  • Press Space to jump and use the hotbar to switch equipment

Tips and Strategies

  • Build a basic bed defense before rushing another island.
  • Carry only the resources you need when crossing exposed bridges.
  • Coordinate upgrades and attacks with teammates instead of fighting alone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bloxd BedWars a team game?

Yes. Players work with a team to defend their bed and eliminate rival teams.
