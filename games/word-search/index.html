About This Game

Word Search hides a list of words inside a field of letters. Words may read in either direction and can appear horizontally, vertically or diagonally.

How to Play

  1. Choose a word from the list to keep it in mind.
  2. Tap its first letter, then tap its last letter.
  3. Find every listed word to complete the puzzle.

Controls

  • Tap the first and last letter of a word
  • Words may run horizontally, vertically or diagonally
  • Choose New Puzzle for another grid

Tips and Strategies

  • Search for uncommon first letters before scanning common ones.
  • Check all eight directions from a promising letter.
  • Use word length to rule out short lines near the edge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can words appear backwards?

Yes. Hidden words may run in either direction horizontally, vertically or diagonally.
