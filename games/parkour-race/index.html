About This Game

Parkour Race is a 3D stickman running game by Madbox. Steer across rooftops, vault obstacles and find boosts while racing a crowd toward the finish line. Gameul provides a guide and links to the game on Poki.

How to Play

  1. Open Parkour Race on Poki.
  2. Steer your runner left and right to line up a clear route across the rooftops.
  3. Use boosts and keep your momentum to reach the finish first.

Controls

  • Press A or the left arrow to move left
  • Press D or the right arrow to move right
  • On mobile, follow the game's touch controls

Tips and Strategies

  • Look ahead for a clear landing area before changing direction.
  • Aim for speed boosts when they fit your route.
  • Use small steering adjustments near the edges of a roof.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I control Parkour Race?

Use A and D or the left and right arrow keys to steer. Follow the touch controls when playing on mobile.
