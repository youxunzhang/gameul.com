About This Game

Dreadhead Parkour is a platform game by Gametornado. Each level combines jumps, slides and dangerous traps that reward careful timing. Gameul offers controls and tips before you continue to Poki to play.

How to Play

  1. Open Dreadhead Parkour on Poki.
  2. Run through each level and jump over gaps, spikes and saws.
  3. Slide under low obstacles and collect stars while heading for the exit.

Controls

  • Press A to run left and D to run right
  • Press W to jump and S to slide
  • On mobile, follow the on-screen controls

Tips and Strategies

  • Watch a moving hazard's rhythm before committing to a jump.
  • Prepare to slide before you reach a low passage.
  • Learn the route first, then return for harder-to-reach stars.
