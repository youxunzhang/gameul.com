About This Game

Bloxd Skyblock is a floating-island building mode provided by Bloxd.io. Begin with a compact island, use limited materials carefully and develop a larger home in the sky while playing alongside the Bloxd community.

How to Play

  1. Open Skyblock and choose an available lobby on the official Bloxd.io screen.
  2. Collect the starting resources without dropping important blocks into the void.
  3. Expand the island gradually and use new materials to improve the build.

Controls

  • Use WASD or the on-screen controls to move
  • Use the mouse or touch controls to mine and place blocks
  • Press Space to jump and use the inventory to craft

Tips and Strategies

  • Crouch or work from a safe position near the island edge.
  • Turn renewable resources into a stable supply before decorating.
  • Store rare items before attempting risky building work.
