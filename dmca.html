DMCA Takedown Policy

GameUL respects intellectual property rights. If you believe content on GameUL infringes your copyright, please submit a notice with the details below.

Submit a Notice

Email hello@gameul.com with the following:

  • Your full legal name and contact information.
  • A description of the copyrighted work.
  • Links to the material you believe is infringing.
  • A statement that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on behalf of one.
  • Your electronic signature.

Counter Notices

If you believe content was removed by mistake, send a counter notice with sufficient detail for us to review. We comply with applicable laws when processing DMCA requests.
