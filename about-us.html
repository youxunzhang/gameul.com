About Gameul

Learn how Gameul builds a focused collection of free, original browser games for quick and accessible play.

Play a Little, Every Day.

Gameul is an independent HTML5 game site for people who want to play without an install, account or long setup. We build lightweight games that work directly in modern desktop and mobile browsers.

How games are published

Every public game must load correctly, match its title and artwork, include clear controls, and work on supported devices. Gameul original games are developed for this site. Third-party games are published only when their source and commercial usage rights are documented.

Our approach

  • Useful gameplay comes before the size of the catalogue.
  • Game descriptions and instructions are written for the actual game.
  • We do not invent ratings, player counts or popularity claims.
  • Accessibility, responsive design and fast loading are part of every release.
