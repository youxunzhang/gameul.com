Privacy Policy

Read how Gameul uses browser storage, analytics, advertising services and contact information.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Overview

Gameul provides browser-based games without requiring an account. This policy explains information that may be processed when you use the site.

Browser storage

Some games use local storage on your device to remember settings, progress or best scores. This information remains in your browser unless you clear it. Gameul does not use this game data to identify you.

Analytics

Gameul uses Google Analytics to understand general site usage, such as pages viewed, device type and approximate region. Google may process identifiers, IP address and browser information according to its own policies.

Advertising

Gameul uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies to deliver, measure and protect advertising. Depending on your location and choices, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized. Learn more about how Google uses information from partner sites.

Consent choices

Where required, Gameul uses consent controls made available for Google advertising and analytics services. You may also manage or clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings.

Contact

If you have a privacy request, email business@gameul.com. Please do not include unnecessary sensitive information.

Changes

We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The date above shows the latest revision.
