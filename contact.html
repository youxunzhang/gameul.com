How can we help?

For game support, accessibility feedback, licensing questions, privacy requests or business enquiries, email business@gameul.com.

What to include

For a game issue, include the game title, device, browser and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Response times can vary. Gameul does not currently offer telephone support.
